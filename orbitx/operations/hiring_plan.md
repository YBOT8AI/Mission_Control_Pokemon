## Hire #1: Community Manager (IMMEDIATE PRIORITY)

### Role Overview
**Title:** Community Manager  
**Reports To:** COO  
**Location:** Remote (global)  
**Type:** Full-time  
**Salary Range:** $45K-65K/year

### Why This Role First

The Community Manager is the frontline of artist relations. They will:
- Execute 60-70% of outbound outreach (freeing COO for strategy)
- Be the primary touchpoint for artists (first impressions matter)
- Build and nurture the artist community post-onboarding
- Identify promising talent through daily social media engagement

Without this role, the COO becomes a bottleneck—doing both strategy AND execution. This hire unlocks scale.

### Core Responsibilities

**Artist Outreach (50% of time):**
- Identify 50-100 potential artists/week through social media scouting
- Send personalized outreach messages (30-50/day)
- Track responses and manage CRM pipeline
- Schedule discovery calls for COO

**Community Building (30% of time):**
- Moderate artist Discord/Slack community
- Organize virtual events (studio tours, AMAs, workshops)
- Facilitate artist-to-artist connections
- Surface issues/concerns to COO

**Content & Social (20% of time):**
- Manage OrbitX social media (Instagram, Twitter)
- Share artist spotlights and work-in-progress
- Build brand awareness organically
- Coordinate with artists on cross-promotion

### Required Skills & Traits

**Must Have:**
- 2+ years in community management, social media, or artist relations
- Genuine interest in art/creators (not just a job)
- Excellent written communication (warm, professional, human)
- Self-starter—can work independently with minimal direction
- Comfortable with social media platforms (Instagram, Twitter, TikTok)
- Basic understanding of NFTs/crypto (or strong willingness to learn)

**Nice to Have:**
- Art background (BFA, gallery experience, working artist)
- Existing network in art community
- Previous startup experience
- Multilingual (for global outreach)

**Red Flags:**
- Corporate speak, robotic communication
- Only interested in "crypto/NFT space" for money
- No genuine curiosity about art or artists
- Needs constant hand-holding

### Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Artists Identified | 300+ |
| Outreach Messages Sent | 500+ |
| Response Rate | 25%+ |
| Discovery Calls Scheduled | 40+ |
| Community Engagement Rate | 15%+ (Discord active users) |
| Artist Satisfaction | 8/10+ (onboarding survey) |

### Interview Process

1. **Application Review:** Portfolio + cover letter (must show genuine interest)
2. **Written Exercise:** Draft 3 outreach messages to real artists (we provide profiles)
3. **Video Call (30 min):** Culture fit, communication style, art knowledge
4. **Reference Check:** 2 previous managers or colleagues
5. **Trial Week (Paid):** 10-15 hours of actual outreach work, evaluate results

### Where to Find Candidates

- Art school career centers
- Gallery assistant job boards
- Remote work sites (We Work Remotely, Remote OK)
- Twitter/Instagram (look for art community managers already)
- Referrals from artist network

## Hire #2: Operations Coordinator (MONTH 2-3)

### Role Overview
**Title:** Operations Coordinator  
**Reports To:** COO  
**Location:** Remote (global)  
**Type:** Full-time  
**Salary Range:** $40K-60K/year

### Why This Role Second

Once artist pipeline is flowing (thanks to Community Manager), operational complexity increases. The Operations Coordinator ensures nothing falls through the cracks:
- Manages onboarding workflow (10-20 artists/month)
- Handles logistics, scheduling, documentation
- Frees COO to focus on strategy and high-level relationships
- Builds systems and processes for scale

This is the "glue" role that makes the operation run smoothly.

### Core Responsibilities

**Artist Onboarding (40% of time):**
- Guide artists through setup (wallets, profiles, collections)
- Schedule and coordinate education sessions
- Track onboarding progress in CRM
- Troubleshoot technical issues (escalate to Lead Architect as needed)

**Marketplace Operations (30% of time):**
- Monitor listings and sales activity
- Coordinate launch schedules with artists
- Prepare weekly sales reports for COO/CEO
- Manage fee calculations and payouts (with finance support)

**Administrative Excellence (20% of time):**
- Maintain documentation (SOPs, FAQs, guides)
- Schedule meetings, manage COO calendar
- Handle vendor relationships (tools, services)
- Prepare board/investor updates (with COO)

**Cross-Functional Support (10% of time):**
- Liaise with Lead Architect on artist feedback/feature requests
- Support Community Manager on events and initiatives
- Assist COO on special projects

### Required Skills & Traits

**Must Have:**
- 2+ years in operations, project management, or executive assistant role
- Extremely organized (lives in calendars, checklists, systems)
- Proactive problem-solver (anticipates issues before they happen)
- Clear communicator (written and verbal)
- Comfortable with tools (Notion, Airtable, Google Workspace, Slack)
- Detail-oriented without being perfectionist

**Nice to Have:**
- Startup experience (wearing multiple hats)
- Art world exposure (galleries, auctions, artist management)
- Basic finance/bookkeeping knowledge
- Process improvement background

**Red Flags:**
- Waits to be told what to do
- Disorganized, misses deadlines
- Poor written communication
- Resistant to documentation/systems

### Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Artists Successfully Onboarded | 20+ |
| Onboarding Time (avg) | <14 days |
| Onboarding Satisfaction | 9/10+ (artist survey) |
| Operational Errors | <2/month |
| SOPs Documented | 10+ processes |
| COO Time Saved | 10+ hours/week |

### Interview Process

1. **Application Review:** Resume + cover letter
2. **Practical Exercise:** Given a chaotic scenario, organize it into a plan (test systems thinking)
3. **Video Call (30 min):** Communication style, problem-solving approach
4. **Reference Check:** 2 previous managers
5. **Trial Project (Paid):** 1-week engagement on real operational task

## Hire #3: Partnerships & Collections Lead (MONTH 4-6)

### Role Overview
**Title:** Partnerships & Collections Lead  
**Reports To:** COO  
**Location:** Remote (global)  
**Type:** Full-time  
**Salary Range:** $50K-75K/year (plus potential performance bonus)

### Why This Role Third

Once the machine is running (Community Manager sourcing, Operations Coordinator onboarding), we need someone to:
- Elevate the platform through strategic partnerships
- Curate special collections and themed drops
- Build relationships with galleries, institutions, collectors
- Drive premium artist acquisitions

This role moves us from "working platform" to "prestige platform."

### Core Responsibilities

**Strategic Partnerships (40% of time):**
- Identify and pursue partnerships with galleries, art schools, residencies
- Negotiate collaboration terms (revenue share, co-marketing)
- Manage partner relationships ongoing
- Explore brand partnerships (art-adjacent: design, fashion, culture)

**Curated Collections (30% of time):**
- Develop themed collection concepts (e.g., "Emerging Voices," "Regional Spotlight")
- Recruit artists for specific collections
- Coordinate collection launches with marketing
- Ensure quality and coherence

**Collector Relations (20% of time):**
- Build relationships with serious collectors
- Organize collector previews and exclusive events
- Gather collector feedback for platform improvement
- Coordinate with artists on collector requests/commissions

**Market Intelligence (10% of time):**
- Track competitor activity and market trends
- Report insights to COO/CEO
- Identify new opportunities (geographies, mediums, verticals)

### Required Skills & Traits

**Must Have:**
- 3+ years in art world (gallery, auction house, art fair, or artist management)
- Existing network of artists, galleries, or collectors
- Strong negotiation and relationship-building skills
- Understanding of art market dynamics
- Professional presence (comfortable in high-stakes conversations)
- Strategic thinker with execution capability

**Nice to Have:**
- NFT/crypto experience
- MBA or business background
- Multilingual (for international partnerships)
- Writing/speaking experience (panels, articles, thought leadership)

**Red Flags:**
- Snobbish or gatekeeping mentality (we're anti-elitist)
- Only interested in "high art" (we celebrate diverse forms)
- No track record of closing deals/partnerships
- Resistant to technology/digital innovation

### Success Metrics (First 90 Days)

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Partnerships Secured | 3-5 formal agreements |
| Curated Collections Launched | 2+ |
| Premium Artists Onboarded | 10+ (via partnerships) |
| Collector Relationships Built | 20+ serious collectors |
| Partnership Revenue | $10K+ (attributable) |

### Interview Process

1. **Application Review:** Resume + cover letter + portfolio of past work/partnerships
2. **Case Study:** Given a partnership opportunity, outline approach and negotiation strategy
3. **Video Call (45 min):** Art market knowledge, relationship philosophy, culture fit
4. **Reference Check:** 3 references (including at least 1 partner/client)
5. **Meet & Greet:** Virtual coffee with COO + CEO (culture and vision alignment)

## Alternative Approaches

### Option A: Contractor-First (Lower Risk)
- Start all 3 roles as contractors (20-30 hrs/week)
- Convert to full-time after 90 days if working well
- **Pros:** Lower commitment, test fit before full hire
- **Cons:** Less loyalty, divided attention, harder to build culture

### Option B: Hire #1 Only, Delay Others
- Hire Community Manager immediately
- COO handles operations until scale demands more
- **Pros:** Lower burn, CEO/COO retain more control
- **Cons:** COO bottleneck, slower growth, burnout risk

### Option C: Combine Roles #1 & #2
- Hire "Artist Operations Manager" (outreach + onboarding)
- Delay Partnerships Lead to Year 2
- **Pros:** Lower headcount, broader role
- **Cons:** Jack-of-all-trades, may not excel at either

**Recommendation:** Option A (Contractor-First) for all 3 roles, with clear conversion path. Reduces risk while we validate the model.
