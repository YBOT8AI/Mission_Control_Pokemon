# OrbitX NFT - Artist Outreach Strategy

**Document Owner:** COO  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Created:** 2026-04-24  
**Review Cycle:** Bi-weekly

## Executive Summary

**Goal:** Identify, engage, and onboard 100 high-quality unseen artists for beta launch (Q3 2026).

**Philosophy:** Quality over quantity. We're not building a content farm—we're curating a gallery of exceptional work that deserves recognition.

**Approach:** Relationship-first outreach. No cold spam. Every contact is personalized, respectful, and value-driven.

---

## Target Artist Profile

### Who We're Looking For

**Primary Criteria:**
- Exceptional craft in visual/fine arts (Phase 1)
- Limited commercial recognition (<10K followers, no major gallery representation)
- Active creator (produces work consistently)
- Professional attitude, open to collaboration
- Located anywhere (global talent pool)

**Secondary Signals:**
- Engaged small community (loyal followers, not vanity metrics)
- Cross-disciplinary interests (potential for Phase 2 music crossover)
- Previous rejection from traditional gatekeepers (galleries, agents)
- Financial need/opportunity awareness (NFTs as genuine opportunity, not speculation)

**Red Flags:**
- Primarily motivated by quick flips
- Unwilling to engage with platform/community
- History of plagiarized or AI-generated work passed as original
- Toxic behavior in public spaces

## Sourcing Channels

### 1. Social Media Scouting (40% of pipeline)

**Platforms:**
- **Instagram:** Visual arts hub. Search hashtags like #emergingartist, #contemporarypainting, #digitalart, #fineart
- **Twitter/X:** Art community active. Follow #ArtTwitter, #NFTartists (but filter for genuine creators)
- **TikTok:** Process videos, studio tours—shows dedication and skill
- **Behance/Dribbble:** Professional portfolios, often underrepresented talent

**Tactics:**
- Dedicated 2 hours/day for COO or Community Manager
- Save promising profiles to CRM/tracking sheet
- Engage genuinely before outreach (comment, share, build awareness)
- Look for artists with 500-10K followers (sweet spot: talented but overlooked)

### 2. Art School & Program Partnerships (20% of pipeline)

**Targets:**
- BFA/MFA programs (especially state schools, not just elite institutions)
- Community college art departments
- Online art education platforms (Skillshare, Domestika, Coursera)
- Artist residency programs

**Approach:**
- Contact program directors about partnership opportunities
- Offer educational workshops on NFTs/blockchain (value-first)
- Provide platform credits/scholarships for students
- Build long-term pipeline, not one-off recruitment

### 3. Community & Forum Scouting (15% of pipeline)

**Spaces:**
- Reddit: r/Art, r/ContemporaryArt, r/DigitalArt, r/ArtistLounge
- Discord: Art-focused servers, creator communities
- DeviantArt, ArtStation (filter for emerging creators)
- Local art subreddits by city/region

**Tactics:**
- Active participation, not just extraction
- COO or Community Manager as visible community member
- Identify consistent high-quality contributors
- Build reputation before outreach

### 4. Referral Program (15% of pipeline)

**Mechanism:**
- Each onboarded artist can refer 2-3 peers
- Referral bonus: Reduced platform fees for first 3 months
- Quality gate: Referrals still go through curation

**Why It Works:**
- Artists know other talented artists
- Pre-vetted by someone who understands the platform
- Builds community from day one

### 5. Gallery & Curator Relationships (10% of pipeline)

**Targets:**
- Small independent galleries (not blue-chip)
- Curators who focus on emerging art
- Art bloggers/writers who cover underrepresented artists
- Regional art fairs (not Art Basel—smaller, accessible events)

**Approach:**
- Partnership, not competition
- Offer their artists additional revenue stream
- Co-hosted events or features
- Revenue share on referred artist sales (optional incentive)

## Outreach Process

### Stage 1: Initial Contact (Warm Introduction)

**Channel:** DM (Instagram/Twitter) or Email (if available)

**Template Framework:**
```
Subject: Your work + OrbitX opportunity

Hi [Artist Name],

I've been following your [specific piece/series] and [genuine compliment about 
their work—shows we actually looked]. The way you [specific technique/approach] 
is exceptional.

I'm reaching out from OrbitX, a new NFT marketplace built specifically for 
artists like you—exceptional talent that hasn't gotten the recognition you 
deserve. We're not another platform chasing hype. We're curating a gallery of 
serious artists and building real collector relationships.

We're launching our beta in Q3 and would love to invite you as a founding artist. 
No pressure, no spam. Just a conversation about whether this could be a good fit.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call next week? I'd love to hear about your work 
and share what we're building.

Best,
[Name]
COO, OrbitX
```

**Key Principles:**
- Personalized (reference specific work)
- No generic copy-paste
- Clear value proposition
- Low-pressure ask (conversation, not commitment)
- Professional but human tone

### Stage 2: Discovery Call (15-20 minutes)

**Agenda:**
1. Artist shares their story, work, goals (10 min)
2. We explain OrbitX vision and artist benefits (5 min)
3. Q&A, address concerns (5 min)

**What We Learn:**
- Their current sales/channels
- Pain points with existing platforms
- Goals (income, recognition, creative freedom)
- Comfort level with NFTs/crypto
- Timeline and availability

**What We Share:**
- Artist-first philosophy (manifesto)
- Revenue share model
- Platform features (as available)
- Beta timeline and expectations
- Support we provide

**Qualification:**
- Green flags: Professional, open, aligned values, consistent work
- Yellow flags: Skeptical but curious (needs nurturing)
- Red flags: Only interested in quick money, hostile to feedback, unrealistic expectations

### Stage 3: Application & Curation

**Process:**
1. Artist submits portfolio (10-15 pieces)
2. Artist statement (300-500 words)
3. Brief questionnaire (goals, availability, expectations)

**Review Committee:**
- COO (operations fit)
- Lead Architect (technical feasibility)
- Guest curator (rotating, external perspective)

**Decision Timeline:** 5 business days

**Feedback:** All applicants receive response. Rejections include constructive feedback (builds goodwill, reputation).

### Stage 4: Onboarding

**For Accepted Artists:**

**Week 1: Setup**
- Platform account creation
- Wallet setup assistance (we guide, don't assume knowledge)
- Profile completion (bio, statement, contact)
- Upload first collection (5-10 pieces)

**Week 2: Education**
- NFT basics (if needed)
- Pricing strategy workshop
- Marketing best practices
- Platform features walkthrough

**Week 3: Launch Prep**
- Collection review and feedback
- Marketing asset creation (we help with copy, visuals)
- Launch date scheduling
- Collector preview list building

**Week 4: Go Live**
- Collection drops
- Coordinated promotion (our channels + theirs)
- Live support during launch
- Post-launch review and optimization

**Ongoing Support:**
- Monthly check-ins (first quarter)
- Sales analytics and insights
- Marketing collaboration opportunities
- Community events and features

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Low response rate | Medium | High | A/B test messaging, refine targeting, warm up before outreach |
| Artist churn post-launch | Medium | High | Better onboarding, ongoing support, community building |
| Quality dilution (scale too fast) | High | Critical | Maintain curation standards, reject openly, communicate why |
| NFT market downturn | Medium | High | Focus on art + utility, not speculation; build collector relationships |
| Reputation damage (scam accusations) | Low | Critical | Transparency, no hype, deliver on promises, artist-first always |
